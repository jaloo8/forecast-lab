# Institutional methods for a stock, news and options paper study

**Research date:** 2026-09-28. **Decision:** replicate public *research controls*, not firms' private signals. The current evidence is a six-current-survivor, price-only study (1,077 historical cohorts, mixed results); the paper ledger has no matured prospective trades. Neither establishes quick profits or the requested **66% year-over-year gain**. No account access, new feed, dependency, option order or real trade follows from this note.

**The 66% target is an annual return goal, not a win rate.** Its gross/net treatment, leverage, period, drawdown and capacity remain unspecified. A widely repeated Medallion 66% gross annual figure could not be verified from a primary audited return series in this research. A [public federal filing](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2012-01-20/pdf/FR-2012-01-20.pdf) documents Renaissance/Medallion performance-fee mechanics, not a reproducible return series or algorithm. Record net return after costs, annualization basis, drawdown and deployed capital before comparing any outside figure. Do not treat a historical institutional claim as the expected result of this $100 paper pilot.

**Primary research to turn into tests.** [AQR's *Quality Minus Junk*](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Working-Papers/Quality-Minus-Junk.pdf) constructs quality from profitability, growth, safety and payout across broad, size-sorted universes: test any quality overlay only with filing-dated inputs and a sufficiently broad universe, not today's P/E on six survivors. [AQR's *Fact, Fiction and Momentum Investing*](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Journal-Articles/JPM-Fact-Fiction-and-Momentum-Investing.pdf) documents momentum across markets; it motivates a predeclared momentum comparator, but its construction and trading scale do not validate our 5/20/126-session rank. [Bailey and López de Prado's *Deflated Sharpe Ratio*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551) addresses selection across many trials and non-normal returns: log every tested rule/lookback, reserve untouched forward periods, and do not quote an unadjusted best-case Sharpe from the search. These papers supply testable hypotheses and error controls, not a 66% forecast.

## Rigorous data contract before a simulation

**Stock and events.** Store a historical point-in-time universe, including later delistings, symbol/CUSIP changes, splits, mergers and cash dividends. Evaluate both price and total return; the current price-only study omits dividends and selects six stocks known to survive. A news/filing observation needs source URL, immutable raw content/hash, entity match, publication time, first retrieval time, revisions, and a conservative *first usable* time. The [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) provide submissions/XBRL, but a fiscal period end is not its public availability time; the [SEC timestamp FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) distinguishes acceptance, filing and report dates and says exact first website availability is not timestamped. Use `max(publication, retrieval)` with next eligible session, and never retroactively insert today's revised facts. For news, archive the first-observed text; do not infer a trading-time signal from a later search result or headline revision.

**Options.** Stock OHLC cannot reconstruct an option chain. Each decision and mark needs timestamped contract identifier (underlying, put/call, strike, expiration, deliverable/multiplier), bid/ask and sizes, volume/open interest, underlying quote, and chain availability; record fees, spread/slippage, stale/crossed quote exclusions, exercise/assignment, expiration and corporate-action adjustments. IV and Greeks must be captured contemporaneously or calculated only from then-available quotes and an explicit rates/dividend model. A local read-only Robinhood probe of **AAPL 2026-10-16 C250** (contract UUID `7e0f59cf-ad25-4b0d-b35c-a13d419b4e36`) returned 18 daily OHLC records for Sep 1–25 and one bid $89.75/ask $93.35, multiplier 100, timestamped Sep 25 19:59:59 UTC. The quote was stale by the weekend capture; the response supplied no historical bid/ask, IV or historical volume. At that ask, one standard contract would cost $9,335 before fees, so it does not fit the $100 book. This is a real contract probe, **not** a historical options research dataset. [Cboe's historical EOD product](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-eod-summary) lists bid/ask snapshots and offers IV/Greeks as **additional purchased calculations**; an EOD quote is not an intraday executable quote. [OCC specifications](https://www.theocc.com/clearance-and-settlement/clearing/equity-options-product-specifications) say standard equity contracts cover 100 shares, may be adjusted by corporate actions, and are American-style. Even a $1.20 option premium costs $120 per contract before fees; a $0.50 premium consumes $50. Short options and assignment can create obligations far beyond $100. Keep options **research-only** until a real chain source and a separately specified capital/risk model exist. No synthetic Black-Scholes fills from stock closes.

## Test design, in priority order

Probe provenance: raw public-data responses are preserved locally in ignored `data/equities/options-capability-2026-09-28.json`, captured 2026-09-28 04:20:39 UTC, SHA-256 `f367c4eff4af442aae02bf56d3ec0ce01032e50a5dd85c2fa163406a3db801b4`. The capture also contains an active $340 call quote and expired September 18 $340 call history; the [runbook](../STOCK-PAPER-LAB.md#stocknews-workflow-verification-and-options-feasibility--2026-09-28) records their feasibility limits. This is a small capability probe, not a strategy-selected sample or a historical chain archive.

1. **Finish the existing forward stock pilot.** Accumulate fresh captures and mature the immutable medium-horizon forecasts. Compare net paper return, SPY-relative return, drawdown, turnover, cash usage, and a confidence interval for *independent* decision dates. Report missing days and zero-trade days. The 1,077 historical cohorts are non-overlapping **within each horizon/block**, but reuse underlying calendar periods and symbols **across horizons**; do not treat the aggregate as 1,077 independent bets.
2. **Add an event ledger before an event signal.** Snapshot a small, predeclared set of SEC filings and public news with first-seen times. Test a fixed, simple event flag against the locked price-only baseline at the next eligible open. Keep entity-matching failures and events unavailable before capture in the denominator. The SEC offers [keyless APIs subject to fair-access rules](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources); endpoint approval is still required by this repo.
3. **Broaden unbiased history before optimizing.** Acquire delisted-inclusive, corporate-action-aware prices and dated fundamentals. Freeze train/validation/test calendar blocks; use walk-forward fitting and embargo or non-overlapping evaluation for 5/20/126-session outcomes. Compare a small *pre-registered* model family to SPY and a no-signal baseline, with all attempted variants counted. A six-stock/395-bar search over news words, lookbacks, thresholds and option structures will overfit quickly.
4. **Options feasibility study only.** Price one bounded, long-premium contract rule on recorded historical bid/ask chains; impose the actual $100 cash constraint, whole contracts, adverse fills, commissions and complete expiry outcomes. Report how often no liquid/affordable contract exists. Stop if reliable historical chains or adjusted deliverables are unavailable; do not substitute theoretical marks.

Low-cost current tools are pandas, SQLite, the existing Streamlit report and immutable raw snapshots. SEC public data can support a later approved read-only source; historical options quote data may carry a purchase cost. No public whitepaper removes the need for time-accurate inputs, executable prices and prospective validation.
