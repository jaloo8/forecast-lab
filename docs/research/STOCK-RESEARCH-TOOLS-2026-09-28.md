## Buffett, Munger and systematic evidence

Buffett credits Munger with moving Berkshire toward buying a *wonderful company at a fair price*, rather than merely a cheap mediocre business ([1989 shareholder letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html)). Berkshire's [2023 letter](https://berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf) favors durable economics, reinvestment at high returns, trustworthy management and patient ownership. These are **long-horizon business-underwriting questions**, not a rationale for five-day trading or a numeric “Buffett score.” For this pilot, record a short, sourced thesis: business durability, capital returns, balance-sheet risk, valuation assumptions and disconfirming evidence. Keep it separate from momentum ranks until outcomes mature.

AQR's original [Quality Minus Junk](https://images.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Working-Papers/Quality-Minus-Junk.pdf) and [Fact, Fiction and Momentum Investing](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Journal-Articles/JPM-Fact-Fiction-and-Momentum-Investing.pdf) show why quality and momentum are reasonable **hypotheses** to test. Their broad-market historical findings do not validate this six-stock implementation, current prices or the $100 paper book. A quality overlay requires genuinely historical, filing-dated profitability, growth, safety and payout inputs; today's P/E and EPS snapshot cannot be moved backward in time.

Do not interpret a 13F as an immediate Buffett trade alert. The SEC says [Form 13F is due up to 45 days after quarter end](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f); positions may have changed before publication. Use its *public filing time*, if at all, as a delayed context observation.

## Evidence requirements and next experiments

1. **Preserve the current forward baseline.** Refresh genuine completed regular-session bars and evaluate the immutable 5/20/126-session forecasts only after each horizon matures. Compare excess price return and paper P&L with SPY; disclose the small, selected watchlist and paper execution assumption. No profitable-strategy claim from a single snapshot.
2. **Fix research data before factor tests.** Obtain corporate actions and dividend-adjusted total-return series, historical constituents including delisted names, symbol changes, and source/filing timestamps. The current split-adjusted price comparison omits dividends and a current six-stock list has survivorship bias. Never evaluate an observation before both publication and retrieval; retain raw versions and revisions.
3. **Run one predeclared addition at a time.** First compare the existing momentum baseline with a simple, filing-dated quality context on the **medium** horizon; hold the model rule and evaluation period fixed before observing outcomes. Separately test whether timestamped news improves forward decisions. Keep 5- and 126-session conclusions pending enough matured observations. Use matched SPY sessions, costs and missing-coverage counts; report negative results. Avoid repeated thresholds, optimized lookbacks and post-hoc ticker selection on the same historical sample.

Unknowns: Robinhood snapshot reuse/redistribution rights, completeness of historical corporate actions, and cost of any broader vendor feed were **not verified** here. No new dependency, endpoint, credential, data purchase or trade was authorized by this research note.
