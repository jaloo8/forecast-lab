# Twenty-year stock research: data feasibility and evaluation boundaries

Research date: September 30, 2026 UTC. Requested outcome: compare stock formulas over approximately twenty years, identify candidates worth further investigation, then test frozen candidates prospectively with paper money. **A twenty-year price study is feasible for sampled surviving stocks through the existing connector. A twenty-year market-wide test including failed companies, dividends and timed fundamentals is not yet available in this workspace.** No subscription, dependency, account or order was created by this review.

## Three documented external paths

These are candidates for a later access decision, not connected or purchased feeds. Official documentation was checked on September 30. Exact commercial permission, delivery lag, symbol coverage and machine/export rights must be verified before integration.

| Path | Documented useful coverage | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|

[SEP documentation](https://data.nasdaq.com/databases/SEP/documentation?anchor=sample-data). | A promising combined price/fundamental candidate. | **Norgate US Platinum or Diamond** | The package table includes delisted securities and historical index membership; Platinum history begins in 1990. [Package comparison](https://norgatedata.com/stockmarketpackages.php). | A practical price-research candidate with important limitations below. | **CRSP US Stock Database** | Official documentation lists active/inactive securities, permanent identifiers, corporate actions, delisting data, and returns with/without dividends. Exchange coverage predates the requested period. [Official product documentation](https://www.crsp.org/research__trashed/crsp-us-stock-databases/?activetab=docs). | Strong benchmark for inclusive price/return research if access is available. Access and licensing have not been established here; no price is assumed. A stock-return subscription does not automatically supply timed fundamentals, analyst expectations or news. |

Sharadar's SF1 documentation distinguishes **As Reported** dimensions (ARQ/ARY/ART) from **Most Recent** restated data (MRQ/MRY/MRT). The AR view indexes information to the filing date; the provider recommends treating it as available by the following day. ART is not always the sum of four historic ARQ rows because restatement treatment differs. Freeze the dimension and availability policy rather than mixing them. [Nasdaq SF1 documentation](https://data.nasdaq.com/databases/SF1/documentation?anchor=see-also). Sharadar separately advertises history to 1998 and 99% survivorship-bias-free coverage; that is a vendor claim, not complete coverage independently verified by us. [Provider fundamentals specifications](https://sharadar.com/docs/fundamentals).

Norgate documents total-return adjustment choices and stable `assetid` identities, but only current fundamentals/consensus. Corrections are applied without vendor versioning. Crucially, it provides neither delisting reason nor economic terminal return. Its suggested final-bar exit is an assumption, not evidence an order submitted without future knowledge could have filled there. The trial covers only two years. Those limits matter even with delisted histories. Freeze local permitted snapshots and explicitly handle terminal outcomes; do not impute successful last-bar liquidation silently. [Official FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php).

CRSP explicitly defines delisting return from post-delisting value relative to the last trading price, which can include later distributions. It records missing values when sufficient payment evidence is unavailable. Thus even this richer source needs an explicit missing-terminal-outcome policy and sensitivity analysis. Do not count an absent outcome as zero gain/loss or drop the security. [Official calculations guide](https://www.crsp.org/crsp_pdf/crsp-us-stock-indexes-databases-calculations-index-methodologies-guide-flat-file-format-2-0/). CRSP's official pages announce migration to Morningstar in 2026; verify current access with the successor's product channel rather than assuming old academic links confer access.

## Useful free research, with a different purpose

The [Kenneth French Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) provides downloadable factor and sorted-portfolio returns, including momentum, reversal, value and profitability-related portfolios. Its notes explain the 2025 switch from CRSP FIZ to CIZ and different dividend-reinvestment treatment. Save the downloaded vintage and methodology. [AQR's value/momentum dataset](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets/Value-and-Momentum-Everywhere-Factors-Monthly) supplies long/short factor histories beginning in 1972.

These series can test whether mechanisms and combinations behaved differently across regimes without buying individual-stock data. They do **not** reconstruct which stocks our scanner could buy, $100 account fills, turnover costs or historical daily order availability. Do not relabel a factor-series result as a stock-selection backtest or apply the live-account capital constraint to a hypothetical zero-cost long/short factor as if it were an executable instrument.

For filing research, [SEC's public APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) require no API key and expose submissions plus XBRL facts. XBRL was first required in 2009, so this interface alone does not promise uniformly structured fundamentals back to 2006. Preserve filing identities and versions; current aggregate facts require careful period and availability selection. EDGAR filings are a specific event source, not comprehensive historical company news or preannouncement consensus. A twenty-year news strategy needs its own licensed or verified archive and coverage audit; none was established here.

## Recommended staged research protocol

1. **Build the collector and coverage report using existing access.** Split explicit daily requests into bounded chunks; retain raw requests, responses, actual retrieval times and hashes. Verify overlaps, source revisions, listing dates, holidays, positive-volume real bars and security identity. Report each symbol's actual coverage. Label the initial current-survivor study exploratory and price-only. Never import historical study cohorts into the A8 forward ledgers.
2. **Create a bounded experiment manifest before testing.** Include economically distinct families and a small number of fixed combinations, with component-only comparisons. Record formation windows, holding windows, portfolio sizes, turnover rules and 10/25/50 bp cost scenarios separately. Freeze a maximum search budget and keep all tried variants, not only winners. Reuse the [method evidence review](STOCK-METHOD-EVIDENCE-2026-09-30.md); do not extend a paper's result to an unrelated formula by name.
3. **Validate on an inclusive dataset before treating results as an advantage.** Prioritize a Sharadar SEP/SF1 coverage/access sample for integrated price/fundamental research, or Norgate for a price-only implementation with explicit terminal limitations. Use CRSP where licensed access exists, especially to audit delist outcomes. This is a research judgment from the documented coverage, not a purchase recommendation. Keep the dataset decision separate from the paper accounts.
4. **Use chronological evaluation.** A candidate design is development on 2006–2015, sequential annual walk-forward evaluation from 2016–2023, and a separately reported 2024–September 2026 diagnostic slice. Exact boundaries must be frozen before new runs. The existing team has already inspected part of 2026 and older public research can also influence design: calling the whole recent slice an untouched holdout would be false. Training may use only outcomes completed before the next test block; exclude labels crossing boundaries, retain warm-up observations and track every later design change.
5. **Include the economic returns and feasibility.** Preserve dividends, split/share transformations, merger consideration, halt/delist outcomes and cash. Use raw contemporaneous prices for affordability/liquidity checks, with a coherent adjustment ledger for return calculations; future back-adjusted prices must not determine historical $5 eligibility. Stress spreads, delay, turnover and execution assumptions. Never assume twenty-year data resolves contemporary fractional-share or options execution feasibility.
6. **Graduate a bounded shortlist to new prospective paper evidence.** Use fixed ranking/reporting criteria across net return, matched-risk/cash benchmark, drawdown, turnover, costs, stability and missing-data sensitivity. Passing historical checks earns a research candidate label, not real-money approval. Freeze rules and first-observation times; assess fresh 21/63/105-session checkpoints without retroactively inserting orders. Existing nine books remain unchanged unless separately approved.

Twenty years contains more regimes, not twenty years of independent daily experiments. Repeatedly changing formulas until something profits creates selection bias. The research loop can continue, but every failure and change must remain visible, with multiplicity/dependence controls and subsequent fresh evidence. **Stopping because a lucky backtest finally wins is not the success criterion.** The search can legitimately end with no qualifying candidate. No twenty-year test, provider advertisement or historical factor return guarantees fast profit or a particular annual return.
