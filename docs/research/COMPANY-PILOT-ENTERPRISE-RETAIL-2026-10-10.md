### MSFT — distribution advantage, capital intensity now the constraint

Azure growth of 43% and Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud growth of 14% support an inference of valuable enterprise distribution and switching costs. Counterevidence: annual cash FCF declined despite earnings growth as infrastructure spending rose. Investment gains inflate earnings: FY GAAP EPS $17.95 versus $17.28 excluding OpenAI effects; other gains still remain. Catalyst: management reported over 30 million paid Copilot seats, but seat adoption alone proves neither incremental margin nor durable return on AI investment. Security, competition and infrastructure execution remain risks. [Evidence](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast).

**Paper judgment:** strongest software business-quality candidate here, conditional on valuation and cash conversion. Thesis fails if two successive releases show Azure growth below 25% while trailing cash capex/revenue rises. Require positive trailing FCF growth before upgrading; reject an earnings-only valuation. FY FCF and EPS above are valuation inputs, not forecasts. Conference-call guidance was not separately verified.

### CRM — recurring contracts, weaker earnings quality than the headline

cRPO of $33.5bn (+14%) supports the embedded-workflow thesis; Informatica contributed $0.456bn revenue, so reported growth is not organic growth. GAAP operating income was essentially flat, while $2.613bn investment gains boosted net income. Shrinking shares coexist with substantially increased debt. Management's August guidance was FY27 revenue $46.1–46.4bn, GAAP margin 20.1%, and FCF growth 4–5%; acquisition assumptions matter. Agentforce ARR's definition expanded, weakening like-for-like adoption comparisons. Integration, interest expense and AI substitution threaten returns. [Evidence and guidance](https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Delivers-Record-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Results/default.aspx).

September update verified: [Investor Day deck](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000210/investorday2026.htm), presented 2026-09-16 and [filed 2026-09-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000210/0001108524-26-000210-index.htm), continues to label FY27 revenue/margin/FCF guidance as August guidance; reiterates FY30 revenue target $63bn+ including acquisitions. Management forecasts returning 190% of FY27 FCF and at least 14% share-count reduction: capital allocation claims, not proof of operating growth. Its selected high-growth AI customer cohorts cannot establish portfolio-wide causation.

**Paper judgment:** hold for organic-growth and deleveraging proof. Falsify the improving-franchise thesis if cRPO growth drops below 10% for two quarters or GAAP operating income keeps stagnating despite reported double-digit revenue growth. Reconcile acquired revenue, SBC and investment gains before valuing it; six-month FCF must not be doubled mechanically.

### COST — credible repeat demand and cash generation; valuation still matters

Q4 comparable sales excluding gasoline-price/FX changes grew 6.7%; membership fees were $1.850bn versus $1.724bn. These support a scale/value/renewal moat inference, but fee growth alone does not isolate renewals from fee increases. Thin operating margin exposes profits to wages, merchandise costs and competition. FY EPS was $20.76; Q4 included $0.15 EPS of nonrecurring tariff refunds. [Earnings evidence](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2026-Operating-Results/default.aspx).

Latest verified catalyst: [2026-10-07 September sales release](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-September-Sales-Results/default.aspx), covering five weeks ended 2026-10-04: sales $30.02bn (+13%), adjusted comps +7.6%; holiday timing added slightly over 50bp to sales/comps. No numerical earnings guidance in the selected earnings release.

The [FY26 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm) resolves the cash-flow gap: FCF grew 19.7% calculated, partly helped by inventory turns and supplier payment terms. Management plans approximately $7.5bn FY27 capex versus $6.435bn FY26; do not extrapolate working-capital benefits indefinitely.

**Paper judgment:** quality watch, conditional on valuation in the companion watchlist. Thesis fails if adjusted comps fall below 3% in three consecutive monthly releases alongside declining membership-fee growth; examine renewal rates before attributing any slowdown to moat erosion. Strong operations cannot establish an attractive entry valuation.

### TGT — operating recovery, materially flattered by refunds

Traffic +3.6% and digital comps +8.7% support a convenience/assortment recovery hypothesis, not yet durable pricing power. Tariff refunds added $0.994bn operating income and $1.65 EPS; removing them reduces quarter margin to approximately 5.9% and EPS to $2.46. Management guided annual sales growth around 5% and EPS $9.90–10.90, including that refund. Price cuts and rising spending could erode gains; discretionary demand and markdowns remain risks. [Evidence and guidance](https://corporate.target.com/press/release/2026/08/target-corporation-reports-second-quarter-earnings).

**Paper judgment:** turnaround watch below COST on business durability. Thesis fails if traffic becomes negative in two consecutive quarters or ex-refund operating margin falls below 5%. Guidance less the disclosed refund gives a calculated $8.25–9.25 EPS scenario, not a normalized consensus estimate. Require sustained ex-refund margin recovery and a verified valuation discount before considering a paper entry.
