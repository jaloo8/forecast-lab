# Equity signal data feasibility — 2026-10-10

Status: research proposal, not a validated strategy. Sources checked on 2026-10-10. No paid subscriptions, market-data downloads, API collectors, dependencies, or execution paths were added. This note does not change forecast-lab's weather/NBA production scope or risk gates.

## Finding

A 20-year daily US equity backtest is technically feasible with suitable historical data. A 20-year backtest combining today's social platforms, creators, news coverage, and engagement statistics is **not established** by the available evidence. Treat these as separate studies: historical price/fundamental rules, then a shorter event study and prospective paper record for social/news triggers. No source below establishes a profitable forecasting edge.

## callsbyjmo: evidence checked

Exact-handle searches found a self-described stock trader's [support page](https://buymeacoffee.com/callsbyjmo). It contains no complete trade or recommendation ledger in the content retrieved. The [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/callsbyjmo/) could not be retrieved by the research tool (HTTP 429). No auditable complete performance record was verified in this review. This is an evidence gap, not a conclusion about the creator's ability or conduct.

A fair test requires a chronological collection of all recommendations in a defined interval, including losing, ambiguous, edited, and withdrawn calls where legitimately available. Capture instrument, direction, original timestamp/timezone, actionable entry condition, horizon, exit rule, and source URL. Stock returns cannot validate option-call performance: options additionally require strike, expiry, premium, and historically executable bid/ask. Highlight reels and today's surviving posts are insufficient to estimate a win rate.

## Proposed pilot before paying for data

The following is a proposed study design, not a reported result or authorization to start collectors:

1. Inventory the supplied research and any existing licensed datasets. Convert claims into dated hypotheses with source URLs. Separate modern hindsight from documents actually available at the proposed signal time.
2. Freeze two separate objectives: a short-horizon event screen (for example 5 and 20 trading days) and a long-horizon ranking (for example 126 and 252 trading days). These are holding periods, not an instruction to short-sell.
3. Build a small manually reviewed evidence ledger from accessible original filings and user-provided/appropriately licensed recommendations. Record `published_at`, `retrieved_at`, `as_of`, identity mapping, and retention basis. Do not fill missing historic posts with invented text or current engagement counts.
4. Use existing trustworthy local price data, if present, for a limited descriptive event study. If prices or timestamps are missing, report the missing inputs rather than invent performance. A current-survivor sample can test mechanics but cannot validate a market-wide stock selector.
5. Pre-register deterministic baseline rules, horizons, eligible universe, benchmark, entry delay, costs, and exclusion rules before viewing outcome comparisons. Store every trial. Separate train/validation/test chronologically, purge overlapping return labels at boundaries, and leave a final time block untouched.
6. Compare price/volume alone, filings/events alone, and their combination. Add social/news triggers only once provenance and permitted access are established. Report net excess return, drawdown, turnover, hit rate, tail losses, coverage, uncertainty, and performance by period—not only a winning chart.
7. Begin a prospective paper ledger only after the source route is approved and implemented. Make each signal immutable as of generation, while respecting upstream deletion requirements for underlying content. Maintain source-policy-aware records; an immutable prediction log is not permission to retain prohibited source text.

Multiple tried strategies can make a historical winner appear convincing by chance; ordinary holdout testing alone can be inadequate in investment backtests. The experimental design above is our recommendation, informed by the primary research on [backtest overfitting](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf). A current LLM also may know later outcomes when reading old company names; use it for traceable extraction with human checks, not as proof that it could have forecast those outcomes historically.

## Decision still needed before a full 20-year run

Choose and verify the historical universe/price source, delisting and corporate-action treatment, point-in-time fundamentals, permitted news/social access, and explicit trading assumptions. Until then the truthful deliverable is a reproducible protocol and data gap report. A full 20-year social-enhanced backtest, creator win rate, calibrated probability, and deployable edge remain unproven.
