## Backtesting and leakage boundaries

**Direct:** `agent/backtest/engines/base.py:329–365` delays stock signals by one bar on each symbol's calendar, then normalizes weights. `:175–242,962–976` separates configured warm-up from evaluated bars; `:1240–1243` sizes using opening equity. `:1395–1424` charges terminal slippage/exit costs and replaces the final mark with liquidation cash. These are worthwhile accounting design ideas.

**Direct:** The strategy receives the full loaded historical panel in `base.py:918–957` before the one-bar shift. The inspected source validator at `runner.py:787–838` checks structural/runtime safety and the interface. **Inference:** A shift does not make arbitrary generated signals causal: a future-dependent computation can already be embedded in the signal series. Prefix-invariance checks, feature availability timestamps and a sealed evaluation process are still needed; this review did not establish their enforcement throughout the vendor.

**Direct:** `agent/backtest/validation.py:232–319` calls equal sequential slices of an existing equity curve “walk_forward_analysis.” It does not refit, select rules or rerun an engine on withheld observations. This is a post-hoc window-consistency diagnostic. Importantly, `quant_strategy_desk.yaml:110–113` itself requires an actual separate OOS run and tells the risk auditor to acknowledge when one was not performed.

**Direct:** `validation.py:30–138` permutes the order of the same completed trade P&Ls; `:144–226` bootstraps individual observed return values. **Inference:** Neither creates genuinely unseen market episodes or corrects an unrestricted research search. Individual-return resampling breaks serial dependence. The permutation retains the same total trade P&L and mainly tests path ordering. It uses `TradeRecord.pnl`; `base.py:2152–2175` stores commission separately, so this diagnostic's reconstructed path is not automatically the net equity curve. Its trade-step Sharpe also uses a bar annualization multiplier. Avoid presenting it as calibrated annual net-profit confidence.

**Direct:** There is a separate stricter factor screen: `agent/src/factors/bench_runner_strict.py:317–640` supports paired random controls and explicit train/test IC slices after a configured boundary. A split is optional; controls can explicitly be false. It computes factors on the full supplied panel and ranks full-sample IR/t statistics. **Inference:** This is more than the window diagnostic, but factor IC and a visible full-sample ranking are not a sealed selection experiment or $1,000 net tradable returns. Do not claim the entire repository has no OOS capability.

**Direct:** `agent/backtest/run_card.py:119–164,168–231` records config/strategy hashes, model identity, configured cutoff and source labels. An unknown cutoff is warned; the specific exposure warning tests `end_date < cutoff`. **Inference:** An overlapping historical window or exactly-cutoff date can escape that particular test, and a user-supplied cutoff is not verified proof of a model's training boundary. Preserve provenance, but classify eligibility per observation and retain the known-history limitation; prompt dates cannot erase model knowledge.

**Direct:** `agent/backtest/loaders/fundamentals_loader.py:48–132,170–194` anchors SEC facts on filing dates rather than fiscal period ends, with earliest-filing selection for PIT mode. It normalizes filing timestamps to dates, and its header admits issuer-specific normalization limitations. **Inference:** This is useful as a design pattern, not proof that all providers or intraday release times are PIT-safe. No full loader audit was performed.

## Economics and options scope

**Direct:** Stock base initial cash defaults to **$1,000,000** (`base.py:519–522`). US global equity defaults to **0.01-share sizing**, **zero commission**, **5 bp one-way slippage**, and permits long/short (`global_equity.py:82–114,154–220`). **Inference:** Those defaults do not establish feasibility for the user's $1,000 account or our specific whole-share/cash-only case. Borrowing the engine would require explicit broker-compatible assumptions, spreads, fees, financing/borrow where relevant, delays and fixed AI/data costs. A positive aggregate return is not an every-calendar-year floor.

**Direct:** `agent/backtest/engines/options_portfolio.py:1–18,39–124,251–264,400–423` synthesizes option prices from underlying prices using Black–Scholes and historical-volatility/smile assumptions. This path is not a replay of historical bid/ask option quotes. Defaults include $1m cash, 0.1% proportional commission, multiplier **1.0**, risk-free rate 5%, and optional same-day fills (`:229–249`). Default execution is next close (`:323–341`); enabled model margin checks can reject unaffordable legs (`:425–457`). **Inference:** Useful for explicitly labeled theoretical experiments, unsuitable as evidence of executable historical US listed-option profitability or typical contract-size feasibility for $1,000. It cannot fill ForecastLab's missing real-options-cache requirement.
