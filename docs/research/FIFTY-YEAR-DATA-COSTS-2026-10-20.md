# Fifty-year data: scope, cost and evidence

Checked **October 20, 2026** against the primary sources below. This is a source and cost assessment, not a new backtest or data-quality certification. The accompanying [source receipt](evidence/2026-10-20-long-history/data-sources.json) records reviewed sections and access limits.

**The practical first step has $0 incremental data fees:** test a preregistered set of **200 configurations** on public research-portfolio returns over **1976–2025**, exactly 50 complete calendar years / 600 months. Reuse the existing computer and deterministic research tools. This estimate excludes electricity, existing equipment, researcher time and any existing assistant subscription; runtime has not been benchmarked. No cloud service, model API or paid subscription is necessary for that bounded calculation. Additional costs or windows do not turn configurations into independent algorithms.

**Execution follow-up:** that bounded study subsequently completed all 3,672
registered cost/lag/window accounts in 431.7 seconds of runner time on this
computer, with $0 additional data fees. Download, engineering and independent
audit time are separate. See the [actual results](LONG-HISTORY-RESULTS-2026-10-20.md);
the preceding estimate and source-review scope remain historical context.

The lowest listed Norgate commitment that spans the target start is $433.13, **43.313% of a $1,000 reference account** (arithmetic comparison, not a trading cost estimate). More importantly, the [current licensing FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/faq.php) requires deletion of database exports, snapshots and backups after expiry when renewal is not imminent; derived rules and results may remain. A six-month subscription therefore does **not** buy a permanent reproducible source archive. Keep licensed data private. No subscription was acquired.

Norgate's [coverage table](https://norgatedata.com/data-content-tables.php) gives S&P 500 constituents from March 1957 and DJIA from January 1950, but Russell 1000/2000/3000 only from July 1990 and Nasdaq-100 from October 1993. Delisted coverage excludes OTC-only securities and is explicitly not guaranteed complete. Current fundamentals are not historical as-filed fundamentals; the vendor also revises its economic series. These distinctions matter before claiming a survivor-free, point-in-time stock universe.

## How to use the free history honestly

French assigns industries each June using SIC information and calculates returns for the following July–June. The market series is excess return: reconstruct total market return as **Mkt-RF + RF**, with input units checked. RF switches source in June 2024; treat it as a research cash proxy, not guaranteed broker interest on $1,000. [Industry construction](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/Faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_49_ind_port.html), [factor definitions](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/f-f_factors.html).

The [library notes](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) say historical returns are rebuilt and can change. January 2025 switched from CRSP FIZ to CIZ, including different dividend reinvestment timing; daily delisting handling changed in May 2015. Freeze retrieval time, raw bytes and hashes; retain documented missing-value sentinels as missing. Require all 600 evaluation months and sufficient preceding warmup, with no silent filling. The published aggregate files do not independently validate every constituent's delisting payoff, information-release date or obtainable fill.

Interpret resulting wealth as a **hypothetical allocation among published research portfolios**, with registered costs deducted. It cannot prove a $1,000 account could buy the underlying baskets in 1976. SPY itself began January 22, 1993, so a pre-inception proxy must remain separately labeled. [Issuer evidence](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy). Nominal shares, spreads, minimum commissions, dividend payment timing, taxes and basket replication need a later security-level implementation study.

## Regimes and news

Use fixed descriptive periods and preregistered lagged price/volatility classifications for regime comparisons; report both losses and gains without choosing boundaries after results. NBER turning points are retrospective, so their eventual recession labels can describe outcomes but cannot be inserted as contemporaneously known trading signals. [NBER methodology](https://www.nber.org/research/business-cycle-dating) was available through indexed primary text; direct page access returned 403. For macro signals, [ALFRED vintage data](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/help/downloaddata) separates observation dates from information available on earlier dates. Specific series' vintage depth and release time still require validation.

GDELT's pre-April-2013 event files are organized by **event occurrence date**; subsequent daily files use when the event was found in reporting. Neither is automatically the original publication instant. [Dataset documentation](https://gdeltproject.org/data.html). GDELT2 launched February 19, 2015; the launch page's promised older backfill is not evidence here that it was delivered. [Launch documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-2-0-our-global-world-in-realtime/). GAL's first file is January 2020 and its `date` mixes publisher timestamps with observation times; an old timestamp can accompany newly discovered or republished material. [GAL field definitions](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/announcing-the-gdelt-article-list-rss-feed/).

**Inference for testing:** require documented first availability, immutable captured content, entity mapping and a decision lag. Publication claims, event dates and retrieval dates should be separate fields. A modern headline summary about a historical crash is explanation, not an input that existed before the crash. Use news as a separately registered shorter-history experiment; no 50-year news edge is established.

## Bounded next action and review limits

Proceed with the free aggregate study while preserving existing ETF, nominal-share and options results as separate evidence. Register 200 finite configurations before performance, including selection rules, time windows, missing-data behavior, cost stress, annual-return tests and all failed trials. Compare identical dates; do not describe known history as untouched. Any promising result still needs implementable instruments, funded accounting and independent reconciliation.

Options remain a separate data and lifecycle problem. Pre-March-2023 Databento BBO is subsampled minute data, with event time in `ts_recv` and no valid receive-time interpretation; EOD option quotes likewise do not prove intraday fills or assignment outcomes. [Databento qualification](https://databento.com/docs/knowledge-base/datasets/opra-pillar), [IvyDB scope](https://optionmetrics.com/united-states/). None of the option products reviewed establishes a 1976–2025 chain. No annual options-data cost is invented from a few sample days.

This review read the identified product pages and methodology sections, not entire provider documentation, full academic papers or purchased data. Advertised availability is distinct from verified records. No credentials were read; no authenticated API, vendor acquisition, subscription, installation or strategy run was performed by this source-review task. Prices can change and must be rechecked before any separately authorized purchase.
