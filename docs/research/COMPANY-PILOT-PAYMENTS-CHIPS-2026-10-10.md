## Company judgments and falsifiable watches

**V — strongest demonstrated economics in this group.** Network-effect evidence: processed transactions grew 10% to 71.7bn and constant-dollar cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe grew 12%. Counterargument: incentives grew 18%, faster than revenue, while nine-month OCF declined despite earnings growth. Litigation and merchant bargaining can absorb that apparent pricing power. The latest verified earnings catalyst includes a $0.563bn severance charge and $0.237bn MDL provision; these are costs, not proof of future savings. Paper-watch thesis: broad activity sustains high profitability; reassess if the next two reports show transaction growth below 5% or GAAP margin below 55%. Those thresholds are research rules, not company guidance. [Visa release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316126000103/q32026earningsrelease.htm).

**PYPL — turnaround watch, weaker pricing evidence.** Scale is real: 439m active accounts and payment volume up 10%; however accounts grew only 0.3%, margin fell 171bp, and transaction-margin dollars grew only 1%. That is evidence against assuming a strong monetization moat from volume alone. Management raised FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance to approximately $5.38 while retaining a mid-single-digit GAAP EPS decline; the recovery remains a company forecast. BNPL sale timing makes adjusted FCF ($1.832bn) distinct from ordinary FCF. Paper-watch condition: two reports with nonnegative YoY GAAP operating-income growth and transaction-margin-dollar growth above 3%; continued deterioration falsifies the recovery thesis. Competitive checkout substitution and credit/fraud costs remain risks. [PayPal release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000080/pypl2q-26earningsrelease.htm).

**AMD — strong growth evidence, execution-sensitive watch.** Data Center revenue grew 107% to $6.7bn, supporting demand for EPYC/Instinct and ecosystem relevance; it does not establish durable software lock-in. Prior-year export-related charges of $0.8bn flatter margin comparisons; current investment gains also complicate earnings quality. Management's Q3 guidance is revenue $13bn ±$0.3bn and 56% non-GAAP gross margin. Paper-watch thesis: infrastructure deployments convert into profitable cash generation; reassess after revenue below the guidance floor or two consecutive quarters of negative continuing-operation FCF. Export restrictions, supplier dependence, competitive software ecosystems and customer concentration are key failure paths. [AMD release](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1295/amd-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results).

**INTC — operating recovery, highest accounting/capital complexity.** DCAI revenue grew 59%; manufacturing/product integration is potential differentiation. Counterevidence: Foundry still lost $2.089bn, and its revenue includes internal business. Altera deconsolidation affects comparisons. The net loss includes $12.529bn escrow-share mark-to-market losses; neither that loss nor adjusted EPS alone measures the franchise. Management forecasts Q3 revenue $15.8–16.8bn and GAAP EPS $0.31. Paper-watch condition: meet that revenue range, retain positive GAAP operating profit, and reduce Foundry losses in successive reports; renewed operating losses invalidate the recovery thesis. Debt, dilution, yields and renewed capital spending constrain upside. [Intel release](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1776/intel-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results).

## Valuation and decision boundary

The table supplies earnings, shares, cash and debt inputs, not a completed valuation. For V/PYPL, obtain cutoff-dated prices and trailing four-quarter GAAP earnings/FCF before calculating multiples; Visa's share classes require as-converted treatment and PayPal customer funds must not become surplus cash. For AMD, model cash flow after recurring SBC dilution and deployment spending rather than annualizing this quarter. For INTC, build a segment cash-flow valuation with debt, minority interests and escrow-related obligations; a negative-GAAP-EPS P/E is not useful. No historical repurchase price is substituted for the current price. Conditional research priority: V for durability, AMD for growth conversion, PYPL for margin repair, INTC for turnaround verification. These are independent hypotheses suitable for prospective paper observation, with no claim of alpha or portfolio suitability.
