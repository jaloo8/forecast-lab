# Static review: TraderMonty Claude Trading Skills

Reviewed 2026-10-19. **Recommendation: selectively adapt its research checklists and artifact contracts; do not install the whole collection or connect it to execution.** The inspected code offers useful research organization, a narrow stock backtest adapter, theoretical options calculations, and advisory risk checks. It does not establish a profitable strategy, a reliable autonomous trading desk, or executable returns for a $1,000 account.

## What is worth adapting

The strongest part is the distinction between a research proposal and evidence. The declared research workflow requires historical data, explicit rules, independent checks and metrics tied to the exact candidate; it explicitly requires a separate backtest. Its final review asks for lookahead, survivorship and out-of-sample checks that the scoring CLI cannot establish itself. This is a useful starting contract for forecast-lab's researcher, data auditor and independent reviewer. [Workflow prerequisites](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/workflows/strategy-research-pipeline.yaml#L12-L35), [evidence gate](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/workflows/strategy-research-pipeline.yaml#L111-L140).

Reusable pieces, after local adaptation:

| Piece | Useful contribution | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy draft/schema and bias checklist | Explicit hypotheses, entry/exit rules, costs, validation questions | Freeze and hash the full executable specification before inspecting outcomes; retain rejected trials |
| `backtest-expert` evaluator | Transparent aggregate scoring, break-even arithmetic and hard gates | Treat its score as a rubric; independently compute all inputs from verified ledger/equity artifacts |
| Round-trip accounting helper | Groups scale-in/out fills and includes fees | Reconcile against the engine and account ledger; explicitly report open positions |
| Position sizer | Decimal-based flooring, optional concentration constraints | Add mandatory available-cash, quantity, cost, gap and portfolio limits |
| Pre-trade checklist/journal | Records answers and distinguishes GO, NO_GO and missing inputs | Bind decisions to current account/state/spec hashes; enforce results outside the advisory script |

The [bias checklist](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-strategy-reviewer/assets/bias_checklist.yaml#L1-L54) covers relevant concerns including delistings, point-in-time universe selection, costs, borrow, capacity, multiple testing and chronological evaluation. Having these questions is valuable; answering them requires data and executable checks.

## Backtesting and validation findings

### 1. The orchestrator does not run a backtest

Observed: the pipeline invokes local detection, hints, concept generation, draft design, draft review and export scripts. It is sequential subprocess orchestration. A supplied LLM-ideas file is input, not a demonstrated autonomous multi-model research team. No backtest stage appears in the inspected stage map. [Stage map and runner](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-pipeline-orchestrator/scripts/orchestrate_edge_pipeline.py#L30-L98).

The draft review's `confidence_score` is a weighted heuristic. For example, estimated annual opportunities start from 252 and are reduced by sector/regime/condition counts; text describing planned regime validation receives credit. This is neither an observed trade count nor a statistical confidence level. The separate strategy-reviewer agent also supplies subjective scenario-probability guidance; those percentages should not become calibrated return forecasts. [Opportunity heuristic](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-strategy-reviewer/scripts/review_strategy_drafts.py#L219-L300), [verdict construction](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-strategy-reviewer/scripts/review_strategy_drafts.py#L456-L480), [agent instructions](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/agents/strategy-reviewer.md#L19-L59).

### 2. A revised draft can export an older ticket

Observed control flow:

1. The designer writes both a draft and a pre-generated export ticket. [Designer](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-strategy-designer/scripts/design_strategy_drafts.py#L460-L477).
2. A REVISE decision changes the draft file, including adding/removing conditions or rounding decimal thresholds to integers. [Revision implementation](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-pipeline-orchestrator/scripts/orchestrate_edge_pipeline.py#L232-L263), [review loop](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-pipeline-orchestrator/scripts/orchestrate_edge_pipeline.py#L329-L364).
3. Export prefers the pre-generated ticket when it exists, rather than rebuilding it from the reviewed draft. [Export selection](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-pipeline-orchestrator/scripts/orchestrate_edge_pipeline.py#L190-L223).

Static consequence: when revision changes a draft that subsequently passes, export can select different rules from those just reviewed. The ticket builder also fixes a 20-day holding horizon and copies only stop-loss/take-profit exit fields, leaving other draft exit semantics outside this export. Require a single immutable executable artifact and equality/hash checks across review, backtest and export before adopting this path. Do not adopt automated decimal rounding as strategy validation. [Ticket conversion](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/edge-strategy-designer/scripts/design_strategy_drafts.py#L349-L382).

### 4. The actual stock backtester is narrow, and its output is insufficient for a strict audit

Observed: the `manifoldbt` adapter handles a single long-only asset with limited indicator/spec support. It runs at the close with a configurable signal delay, fraction-of-equity sizing, fixed basis-point slippage and fees. The default delay is one bar, but zero is accepted with a warning. This is not a multi-asset cash ledger or an options replay. [Supported scope](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/SKILL.md#L132-L140), [execution configuration](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/run_backtest.py#L141-L168), [zero-delay validation/warning](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/spec.py#L119-L167).

Two specific limitations matter:

- `slippage_tested` becomes true when **either fees or slippage** are nonzero. Thus a fees-only run satisfies this flag without a slippage/delay stress study. The evaluator awards execution-realism credit and uses this flag in its gate. Separate “costs included” from measured slippage/delay robustness. [Adapter flag](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/run_backtest.py#L178-L185), [score](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/backtest-expert/scripts/evaluate_backtest.py#L194-L200), [hard gates](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/backtest-expert/scripts/evaluate_backtest.py#L411-L447).
- The JSON output contains the specification, aggregate evaluation inputs, round-trip count and a command. It does not export fills, continuous equity marks, year-by-year account returns or input/code hashes. Completed-round-trip statistics omit still-open positions. Such aggregates cannot establish an every-calendar-year return target or reproduce account-level losses. [Output](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/run_backtest.py#L204-L215), [round-trip convention](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/round_trips.py#L97-L122).

At the adapter boundary, CSV handling checks required columns, parses/sorts timestamps and rejects duplicates; it does not establish corporate-action handling, point-in-time membership, complete sessions or valid OHLC relationships. The external engine could add checks, but that was not verified. The inspected adapter test file mocks the engine, so its source is not evidence of verified engine execution. [CSV loader](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/run_backtest.py#L35-L61), [mock-based test](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/manifoldbt-backtester/scripts/tests/test_run_backtest.py#L1-L139).

### 5. The options advisor is theoretical pricing, with documentation mismatches

The complete Python file provides European Black-Scholes prices/Greeks, historical-volatility helpers and optional FMP HTTP helpers. Its executable entry point uses fixed example inputs and simulated prices; it has no argument parser. Therefore the SKILL's advertised `--ticker`, `--stock-price`, `--days` and other command examples do not configure that entry point. No historical option chain, executable bid/ask, assignment, exercise, expiry settlement or cash/collateral replay is implemented there. [Advertised examples](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/options-strategy-advisor/SKILL.md#L39-L52), [actual entry point](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/options-strategy-advisor/scripts/black_scholes.py#L449-L549).

The SKILL also describes a profitable fraction of an arbitrary price grid as a simplified profit probability, without a probability distribution, and contradicts its bounded iron-condor example by calling the tested-side risk unlimited. These are reasons to independently review educational prose rather than importing it as trading logic. [Grid/probability wording](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/options-strategy-advisor/SKILL.md#L290-L340), [conflicting example](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/options-strategy-advisor/SKILL.md#L482-L514).

## Risk and $1,000 feasibility

Observed: the stock sizer floors quantities and optionally applies position/sector caps. There is **no mandatory cash/notional cap**. From its formula, a $1,000 account, $100 entry, $99.90 stop and 1% planned risk yields 100 shares, or $10,000 notional, unless an additional cap is supplied. This is arithmetic from the implementation, not an executed trade or performance example. The stop-distance risk also does not model gap losses or trading costs. [Formula](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/position-sizer/scripts/position_sizer.py#L79-L96), [optional caps](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/position-sizer/scripts/position_sizer.py#L142-L200).

The drawdown circuit breaker aggregates **realized** daily/weekly/monthly P&L against an operator-supplied account size. It is not continuous marked-to-market peak-to-trough drawdown. Missing or empty state is allowed, while malformed/duplicate state generally halts. An integration must distinguish a genuinely new account from a wrong/missing path and reconcile open exposure before allowing risk. [Empty-state handling](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/drawdown-circuit-breaker/scripts/check_circuit_breaker.py#L304-L346), [calculation](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/drawdown-circuit-breaker/scripts/check_circuit_breaker.py#L555-L733).

The pre-trade gate handles missing recommendation files and invalid risk numbers conservatively, but reads recommendation tokens without verifying their freshness, account identity or source hash. It accepts declared plan/risk values; it does not independently calculate broker buying power. By default a valid NO_GO result still exits zero; callers must parse the result or use `--fail-on-non-go`. [Recommendation reader](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/pre-trade-discipline-gate/scripts/check_pre_trade_discipline.py#L279-L315), [checklist](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/pre-trade-discipline-gate/scripts/check_pre_trade_discipline.py#L353-L382), [exit behavior](https://github.com/tradermonty/claude-trading-skills/blob/cb0f3aadffed6d1089db4b224162c964368baa46/skills/pre-trade-discipline-gate/scripts/check_pre_trade_discipline.py#L654-L714).

For a $1,000 reference account, 0.5% and 1% risk budgets are $5 and $10. This makes integer quantities, minimum tickets, spread/fees and idle cash material. The repository's fractional-share calculator can illustrate rounding but cannot establish broker support or fillability. Options feasibility still requires actual contract-level data, whole-contract sizing and cash/collateral/assignment accounting; theoretical prices cannot substitute for those inputs. No inspected result establishes that an options policy is feasible or profitable at this account size.

## Adoption decision

Use a small research team around deterministic local engines: a coordinator freezes trial budgets and artifacts; a researcher proposes a preregistered hypothesis; a data auditor checks timestamps, coverage and provenance; a backtest worker runs the unchanged executable spec; an independent reviewer challenges evidence and accounting. Risk review follows the same artifacts. Agents should not grade their own rewritten strategy as newly unseen evidence.

Before any pilot, require one candidate ID tied to code/data/spec hashes, explicit chronological partitions, a complete trial ledger including failures, continuous equity and calendar-year tables, cost/delay scenarios, actual $1,000 cash constraints, and machine-readable rejection reasons. Keep research decisions distinct from a paper broker's acknowledgments, fills and reconciliation. More agents can distribute checks; they cannot create missing historical data or turn a rubric score into predictive evidence.

Strategic API use should begin with reusable cached inputs and offline computation. Identify the actual provider, remaining allowance, expiry, endpoint entitlements and intended request before assigning any nonzero budget. Count retries and fallback calls, reserve a shared request/token/currency budget before sending, cache by full request identity, and stop at the cap. Treat “free tier sufficient” claims in this repository as unverified for the user's account. No credit balance or paid permission was established by this review.
